Appendix: Data Sources

Annual Production and Monthly Production figures above are built from Casey's internal weekly raw-milk KPI log (Raw Milk Production – Weekly KPI), not the external sources below — those cover the industry-benchmark figures cited elsewhere on this page.

Weekly Production chart — methodology

Plots the weekly "Actual" readings from Casey's raw-milk KPI log directly, one point per week, with no monthly rollup — each of the two lines is a straight 52-week trailing-twelve-month window (TTM Jul 2026 = weeks of 8/4/25–7/31/26; TTM Jul 2025 = weeks of 8/4/24–7/26/25), aligned week-for-week starting the first week of August. That sheet's "Actual" column is labeled pounds but is actually gallons (confirmed against the KPI's own 2026 target note: 709,500 lbs ≈ 82,500 gal, and 82,500 is what's in the sheet); cwt is derived from it at 8.6 lbs/gal, matching the sheet's own cwt column. This replaces an earlier version that summed weeks into calendar months, which produced artificial bumps in any month that happened to catch 5 weekly readings instead of 4.
